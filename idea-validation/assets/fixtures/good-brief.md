# Idea: Shared grocery list for flatmates

## Problem
Flatmates buy the same items twice and run out of basics, because nobody knows what the others bought.

## Target user
People aged 20 to 35 who share a flat with two to five other people.

## Value proposition
One list that everybody in the flat updates. A "bought" tap clears the item for everyone.

## Riskiest assumptions
- A1 [critical] [problem]: Flatmates lose money or time to duplicate or missed purchases every week (test: 10 interviews with people who share a flat; metric: interviewees who report a weekly duplicate or missed purchase; pass: >= 6; kill: < 3; by: 2026-11-30)
- A2 [critical] [demand]: Flatmates sign up before the app exists (test: landing page with a sign-up form, shared in two flat-share groups; metric: sign-ups from 200 visitors; pass: >= 20; kill: < 5; by: 2026-12-15)
- A3 [normal] [channel]: Flat-share groups allow posts about a new app (test: read the rules of five flat-share groups; metric: groups that allow such posts; pass: >= 2; kill: < 1; by: 2026-11-15)

## Experiments
- Interviews: the human talks to 10 people who share a flat about last month's shopping. Tests A1.
- [outward] Landing page: the human publishes a one-page site with a sign-up form and shares it in two groups. Tests A2.
- Desk check: read the posting rules of five flat-share groups. Tests A3.

## Out of scope
- Payments between flatmates.
- Recipe or meal planning.
