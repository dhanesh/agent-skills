# Decision brief

## The Decision

Should we build our own KYC/onboarding stack or renew the vendor contract?
Decide by 2026-08-29, when the vendor renewal auto-extends.

## Situation

The vendor's per-verification price rises 40% at renewal.[^2] Onboarding drop-off
at the KYC step is our largest single funnel loss.

## Options on the Table

- Build in-house end to end
- Renew the vendor on the quoted terms
- Renew for one year while running a document-requirement experiment
- Keep the vendor for identity, bring document capture in-house

## Evidence

KYC-step drop-off is 23% of started applications.[^1]
The renewal quote is 41 per verification, up from 29.[^2]
Verification volume ran 51,000 in FY26.[^1]

## Reference Class

- A lending peer built in-house and took 14 months to reach parity.[^3]
- A second peer split the stack (vendor identity, own documents) and cut drop-off
  by a third in one quarter.[^3]
- A third abandoned an in-house build after 9 months and returned to a vendor.[^3]

## Open Uncertainties

Whether drop-off is caused by the vendor's UX or by document requirements we set
ourselves. Nobody has run the experiment that separates them.
