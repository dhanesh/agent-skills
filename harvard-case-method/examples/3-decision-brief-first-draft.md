# Decision brief

## The Decision

Should we build our own KYC/onboarding stack or renew the vendor contract?
Decide by 2026-08-29, when the vendor renewal auto-extends.

## Situation

The vendor's per-verification price rises 40% at renewal. Onboarding drop-off
at the KYC step is our largest single funnel loss.

## Options on the Table

- Build in-house
- Renew the vendor

## Evidence

KYC-step drop-off is 23% of started applications.
The renewal quote is 41 per verification, up from 29.

## Reference Class

- A lending peer built in-house and took 14 months to reach parity.

## Open Uncertainties

Whether drop-off is caused by the vendor's UX or by document requirements we set
ourselves.
